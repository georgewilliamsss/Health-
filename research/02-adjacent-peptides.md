# Adjacent Peptides and Alternatives: Background Research

**Date:** 2026-10-10

**Subject:** Other peptides and related options that came up while researching the five peptides suggested by a local GP in Columbus, Ohio (GHK-Cu, TB-500, BPC-157, CJC-1295 no DAC and ipamorelin), and which of them are worth raising with the GP.

**Purpose:** Background research behind a one-page purchasing decision. It is not medical advice. The companion file `01-peptide-science.md` covers the five requested peptides in depth.

> **How this was researched.** The analysts could not open web pages from the research environment, so every citation here is search-verified, not page-verified. Claims come from search-result summaries of the cited URLs. Where a fact had no surfaced source, it is marked "unverified" or "general knowledge".

## Evidence grade key

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | FDA-approved, or multiple RCTs for some indication |
| B | Human RCTs exist but are negative, small, or not for general-health use |
| C | Small or limited human data only |
| D | Preclinical (animal or cell) only |

## Top additions worth raising with the GP

Ranked as in the research. Items 1 and 2 are substitutions: they replace a requested peptide rather than adding a sixth. Items 3 to 5 apply only in specific circumstances. Item 6 is a safety step, not a product.

1. **Sermorelin (or tesamorelin) via a licensed 503A pharmacy prescription, in place of grey-market CJC-1295**
   - *Why:* Same GHRH mechanism as CJC-1295 no-DAC. Sermorelin is reportedly compoundable with a prescription, and tesamorelin is FDA-approved (Egrifta), so either comes through a pharmacy-controlled supply chain instead of 'research use only' vials. This replaces CJC rather than adding a sixth injectable.

2. **Topical GHK-Cu (cosmetic serum) in place of, or before, injectable GHK-Cu**
   - *Why:* The human evidence for GHK-Cu is almost entirely topical skin trials. Injectable use is 'ahead of the science' (NPR, Sep 2026). A serum gives the evidence-backed benefit without injection risk.

3. **MOTS-c (metabolic), only if the GP has a specific metabolic goal**
   - *Why:* The only adjacent candidate with a distinct mechanism and some human signal (CB4211 analog, Phase 1b). PCAC voted 7-5 in favour of compounding in July 2026, so a pharmacy route may open after FDA rulemaking. Native MOTS-c has no human data.

4. **Thymosin alpha-1, only if the 'immunity' goal is specific**
   - *Why:* The most human RCT data of the immune peptides (approved abroad as Zadaxin). Sepsis meta-analysis signals are weak in high-quality trials, and there is no evidence in healthy adults.

5. **FDA-approved GLP-1 (Zepbound/Wegovy), only if BMI or cardiometabolic risk warrants**
   - *Why:* Grade-A outcome evidence, but only for overweight or cardiometabolic patients. A legitimate self-pay route exists at \$299-449/month. Avoid research-chemical semaglutide, tirzepatide or retatrutide, which were 2026 FDA enforcement targets.

6. **Baseline labs plus a cancer-screening discussion before any GH-axis or angiogenic peptide**
   - *Why:* BPC-157, TB-500 and the GH secretagogues all carry theoretical tumour-growth concerns. GH/IGF-1 raising also affects glucose. IGF-1, HbA1c/fasting glucose and age-appropriate cancer screening are the safety-relevant 'addition'.

### Where this file and file 01 differ

- **Grades for the growth-hormone pair.** The baseline row here grades ipamorelin and CJC-1295 no-DAC together as C. File 01 grades each molecule D for the claimed general-health benefits. File 01 also says the narrow claim that ipamorelin acutely raises GH would rate about C, and that pooled class evidence for GHRH analogues is about C for raising GH/IGF-1. The C here is not an upgrade.
- **BPC-157 knee-pain report.** The baseline row here says "7 of 12 patients reporting relief". File 01 describes the 2021 Lee & Padgett chart review as 16 patients, 12 on BPC-157 alone, with about 87.5% reporting improvement. The two summaries could not be reconciled. Both rest on search summaries.
- **Prices.** The BPC-157 and TB-500 price ranges here are different snapshots from those in file 01 (for example, about \$27-53 per 10 mg BPC-157 here vs \$39.99-\$97.00 on one aggregator in file 01). Use file 01 for the more detailed listings.
- **CJC-1295 and ipamorelin regulatory status.** The baseline row here records that sources conflict on whether both came off FDA Category 2 in April 2026. The individual peptide research in file 01 found that neither was among the 12 peptides removed in April 2026. Both were removed from Category 2 earlier, in September 2024. The PCAC voted 0-12-1 against adding ipamorelin on 29 October 2024, and FDA proposed against adding CJC-1295 at the 4 December 2024 meeting. Neither is on the 503A bulks list.
- **Blends.** The GHK-Cu research in file 01 calls the GLOW blend a cost-efficient way to buy three of the five peptides if the GP endorses the fixed 50:10:10 ratio. The notes below favour separate vials. Both views are in the research.

## Avoid or low value

- **MK-677 (ibutamoren).** Raised glucose and HbA1c, and a congestive heart failure signal in an elderly trial. Development discontinued. Redundant with ipamorelin.
- **Hexarelin, GHRP-6, GHRP-2.** Less selective than ipamorelin (cortisol, prolactin, hunger) with tachyphylaxis. Fully redundant with ipamorelin.
- **Dihexa.** No human studies, a theoretical c-Met tumourigenesis concern, and reported retractions of key papers.
- **FOXO4-DRI.** Mouse data only, no human safety data, expensive.
- **Retatrutide, grey-market semaglutide or tirzepatide, cagrilintide, tesofensine.** Unapproved or of uncontrolled quality. FDA 2026 warning letters target research GLP-1 sellers. Approved alternatives exist if medically indicated.
- **AOD-9604.** The 24-week Phase 2b (n=536) failed to show weight loss.
- **DSIP.** Weak, dated evidence. The only peptide PCAC voted against in July 2026.
- **Cerebrolysin.** No mortality benefit in stroke and a possible non-fatal SAE increase (Cochrane). Not for healthy adults.
- **Klotho, Humanin, Pinealon, P21, 5-Amino-1MQ.** No human data, and product identity is uncertain.
- **Larazotide, ARA-290, VIP/aviptadil, LL-37 (systemic), kisspeptin-10, oxytocin, PT-141.** Disease-specific, failed, or situational. Not general-health agents.
- **Taking TB-500 and BPC-157 together long-term.** Overlapping 'repair' claims, both with preclinical-only evidence and angiogenic or cancer-theoretical concerns. Redundant for a healthy adult without an injury.
- **Pre-mixed blends (GLOW/KLOW/Wolverine).** No combination studies. Fixed ratios prevent titrating or stopping one component if a side effect occurs. Composition varies by vendor. Buying separate vials also gives one COA per compound and per batch.

## Notes

### Key regulatory facts as of 2026-10-10

Secondary legal and industry sources agree on the following.

- **(a) April 2026 removal.** On 15 April 2026 FDA announced the removal of 12 peptides from 503A Category 2 after the nominations were withdrawn: BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, MOTS-c, Semax, Epitalon, DSIP, Melanotan II, LL-37, PEG-MGF, injectable GHK-Cu and Dihexa. Removal is not authorisation to compound.
- **(b) July 2026 advisory votes.** The PCAC met on 23-24 July 2026. It voted to recommend BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, MOTS-c, Semax and Epitalon for the 503A bulks list, and voted down DSIP. The votes are advisory. HHS/FDA rulemaking is pending. A second PCAC meeting on the remaining five is expected by February 2027.
- **(c) CJC-1295 and ipamorelin.** Their status conflicts across sources. Neither is on the 503A bulks list.
- **(d) Warning letters to research-peptide sellers.** Seven letters were published on 7 April 2026 (GLP-1 focused). Five more were dated 24 August and posted 1 September 2026 (Peak Performance Peptides, Royal Peptides, NuScience Peptides, Peptide Partners, Tex Peptides). FDA reportedly cited the sale of bacteriostatic water alongside peptides as evidence of intended human injection ("the means to prepare an injectable drug for human administration"). This is directly relevant if the buyer wants to buy bacteriostatic water from the same site. Vendors that do this are higher enforcement-risk and could disappear mid-supply. Peptide Sciences reportedly ceased operations on 6 March 2026 (weak source: grokipedia).
- **(e) Sport testing.** All GH secretagogues, BPC-157 and TB-500 are WADA-prohibited. This matters only if the buyer competes in tested sport.

### Quality

Secondhand claims say online research peptides frequently have incorrect content or endotoxin (figures unverified). Vendor certificates of analysis (COAs) are self-reported. A vendor comparison should prefer per-batch COAs from an ISO 17025 lab that include identity (MS), purity (HPLC), endotoxin and sterility, not purity alone.

### Stack versus separate vials

The evidence favours separate vials. There are no blend studies. Separate vials allow independent dosing and stopping, and each gets its own per-batch COA.

The two evidence-supported substitutions to raise with the GP are:

- A prescription GHRH analogue (sermorelin or tesamorelin) in place of CJC-1295.
- Topical GHK-Cu in place of, or before, injectable GHK-Cu.

Five unapproved, research-only injectables for "overall health" have no outcome-trial support. Where pharmacy or prescription routes exist, they should be pointed out.
