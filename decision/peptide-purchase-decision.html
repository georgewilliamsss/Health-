Why this vendor

  • Per-product COAs: each product has a distinct Freedom Diagnostics accession, searchable at koipeptides.com/coa/; HPLC 99.1-99.96%; TB-500 shows LC-MS identity and net 9.97 mg.
  • Beats Kylo, narrowly: Kylo puts lot SD-260728-1 on 6+ compounds, relies on a weak lab (Optiq, 2/10) for TB-500 and ipamorelin, and shows "COA coming soon" on current batches.
  • Same-site water and fast shipping: standalone 3/10 mL water; same-day dispatch, 3-5 day transit, free over $200; no FDA warning letter found.
  • Gaps: one lab only (Freedom, 4/10, no ISO 17025 found); no Finnrick or TitrateLab data; no endotoxin value seen for any lot; Koi's own lot IDs are not published.
  • Red flags: unverified Trustpilot claims of a relabelled COA and non-working QR codes; PeptIQ 65, "Mixed signals"; an overfill report; BPC-157 purity differs between pages.

Separate vials, not a stack

Buy GHK-Cu, BPC-157 and TB-500 as single vials, one lot per peptide for the whole course. Koi sells no standalone CJC-1295 no DAC, so its 1:1 5/5 mg blend is the only acceptable fallback, and only if its lot PDF gives MS identity, quantity and purity per component plus endotoxin; if not, switch vendor. Reject GLOW, KLOW, Wolverine (BPC/TB) and the GLOW Stack.

Before you order

  • Go/no-go gates: checkout accepts a 432xx address; BPC-157 10 mg is listed; Koi emails full PDFs for the shipping lots (chromatogram, MS mass, net content, endotoxin in EU) and confirms one lot per peptide in writing. Masses: GHK-Cu ~402, BPC-157 ~1,419, TB-500 frag. ~889, CJC no DAC ~3,368 (DAC is ~3,647), ipamorelin ~712 Da.
  • Legal status: research-use-only; none of the five is FDA-approved; no lawful injectable route today. FDA letters (Mar, Jun, Aug 2026) treat vendor-sold water as evidence of human-use intent. Lawful GP options: topical GHK-Cu (503A pharmacy); sermorelin or tesamorelin for CJC/Ipa.
  • On arrival: look up each accession at freedomdiagnosticstesting.com (client must read Koi); match the lot printed on each vial to its COA, and raise any unlabelled vial before use. Unopened vials frozen, mixed at 2-8 C; discard 28 days after first puncture.
  • GP sets dosing: doses in mcg and syringe units; stagger starts, one agent every 5-7 days. Baseline IGF-1, HbA1c, BP, copper and ceruloplasmin; review at 4 weeks; stop on high IGF-1, rising glucose, oedema, infection or cloudy GHK-Cu.
  • Water: pharmacy USP bacteriostatic water (GP prescription) is safer; if you accept it, Panda (6/10) outranks Koi.
  • Keep the first order small: 8 weeks costs about $679.30 (GHK-Cu 2, BPC-157 3, TB-500 3, blend 2, water 4), given FDA enforcement waves and vendor closures. Pay by credit card; no refund policy found.
